- Choose which of Google, Yandex, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Brave, and Ecosia appear as icons in the search bar.
- View a seven-day weather forecast with the city name, or keep a compact summary beside the clock.
- Switch search engines without losing the query or cursor position.
- Remove the persistent saved-status message from the footer.
