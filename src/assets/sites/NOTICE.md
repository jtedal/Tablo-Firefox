# Bundled site icons

`figma.svg`, `github.svg`, `gmail.svg`, `notion.svg`, `reddit.svg`, `telegram.svg`, `twitch.svg`, `vk.svg`, `wikipedia.svg`, and `youtube.svg` come from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/4d29a35c954699b389a834aacc97dd6e7e74143c), a CC0 project. Their fill colors were adjusted for the default tiles. Brand names and logos belong to their respective owners; the extension is not affiliated with these services. See the project's [license](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/4d29a35c954699b389a834aacc97dd6e7e74143c/LICENSE.md) and [icon disclaimer](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/4d29a35c954699b389a834aacc97dd6e7e74143c/DISCLAIMER.md).

`amazon.ico` is the favicon supplied by https://www.amazon.com/favicon.ico. `ozon.png` is Ozon's favicon retrieved from Google's favicon cache at https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=ozon.ru&sz=128. These are bundled for identifying the corresponding default bookmarks; their brands retain their rights.

The existing `../google.svg` and `../yandex.svg` assets are also used for their default site tiles.
